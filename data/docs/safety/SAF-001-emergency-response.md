# Emergency Response for Entrapment and Serious Injury

Calder Ridge Manufacturing, Plant 1

## 1. Purpose and scope

This procedure applies to every employee, contractor and visitor on Lines 1, 2 and 3. It covers the first actions when a person is caught in, struck by or trapped against a machine, including presses, CNC machines and conveyors. Stations are numbered along each line, and every station has a red emergency stop (E-stop) within reach of the operator position.

## 2. Emergency contacts

| Contact | Extension | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Plant emergency line | 2911 | Answered 24 hours a day by the security control room |
| First aid room | 2920 | Staffed on day and swing shifts |
| Maintenance on call | 2450 | Electrical and hydraulic support |
| EHS manager | 2305 | Notify once the injured person is safe |

Dial 2911 from any plant telephone. Post this table at every line entrance and in every supervisor station.

## 3. Person caught in a machine

Act in this order. Do not wait to confirm how serious the injury is.

1. Press the nearest emergency stop (E-stop). If you are not sure which one is nearest, press any E-stop on that line.
2. Call the plant emergency line on extension 2911. Give your name, the line and station number (for example, "Line 3, Station 3") and state that a person is caught in a machine.
3. Stay with the injured person and keep them calm. Tell them help is on the way and ask them not to pull against the machine.
4. Send a second person to the nearest building entrance to meet the ambulance and guide responders to the station.
5. Do not restart, jog or reverse the machine to free the person. Do not remove guards or cut away machine parts unless trained responders direct you to.
6. Do not move the injured person unless there is immediate danger such as fire or a fluid leak onto a hot surface.
7. Notify the shift supervisor as soon as a second person is with the injured person.

## 4. After the machine is stopped

Maintenance on call (extension 2450) applies lockout/tagout under SAF-002 before anyone releases the person. Only trained maintenance staff, working with the emergency responders, may release stored energy. On Press 4 this includes blocking the ram and bleeding the accumulator.

Leave the machine and the area as found. Do not clean up, restart the machine or move tools until EHS releases the scene.

## 5. Other emergencies

For a fire, sound the alarm, evacuate by the nearest exit and assemble at muster point A (north car park) or muster point B (east gate). For a chemical splash to the eyes or skin, go to the nearest eyewash or shower and follow SAF-005.

## 6. Reporting and investigation

The shift supervisor files an incident report within 1 hour of the event. EHS begins the investigation within 24 hours. The machine stays locked out until EHS and the maintenance manager both release it in writing.
