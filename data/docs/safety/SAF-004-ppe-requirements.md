# Personal Protective Equipment Requirements

Calder Ridge Manufacturing, Plant 1

## 1. Purpose and scope

This procedure lists the personal protective equipment (PPE) required on the production floor and for specific tasks. It applies to employees, contractors and visitors on Lines 1, 2 and 3.

## 2. Minimum PPE on the production floor

Everyone on the floor wears:

- safety glasses with side shields (ANSI Z87.1);
- steel-toe boots rated to ASTM F2413;
- hearing protection in areas marked above 85 dBA, including all of Line 3 while Press 4 is cycling;
- close-fitting clothing with no loose cuffs, scarves, jewellery or dangling lanyards near moving parts.

## 3. Cleaning hydraulic presses

Cleaning Press 4 (the hydraulic press on Line 3) exposes the worker to hydraulic oil, solvent degreaser and sharp edges on dies and guards. Lock out the press under SAF-002 before starting.

Required PPE for cleaning Press 4:

1. splash goggles (ANSI Z87.1 D3) worn over or instead of safety glasses;
2. nitrile gloves with a long cuff, minimum 8 mil thickness;
3. oil-resistant steel-toe boots;
4. a long-sleeved coverall or apron that covers the forearms.

Add a face shield over the goggles when using the pressurized solvent sprayer. Bag used wipes and absorbent pads in the labelled oily waste container. Replace nitrile gloves that are torn, swollen or soaked through.

## 4. Task PPE summary

| Task | Additional PPE |
|---|---|
| Changing dies on Press 4 | Cut-resistant gloves (A4), foot guards |
| Grinding or deburring | Face shield, cut-resistant sleeves |
| Handling coolant concentrate | Splash goggles, nitrile gloves, apron |
| Working near Conveyor C2 | High-visibility vest when forklifts operate nearby |

## 5. Inspection and replacement

Inspect PPE before each use. Damaged, contaminated or expired equipment is taken out of service and replaced from the crib at no cost to the employee. Safety glasses and goggles with scratched lenses are replaced immediately.

## 6. Issue and training

Supervisors confirm that each new employee is issued the PPE for their area and trained in its use, limits and care before starting work. Training records are kept by EHS.
