# Chemical Handling and Spill Response

Calder Ridge Manufacturing, Plant 1

## 1. Purpose and scope

This procedure covers the storage, handling and spill response for the chemicals used on the production lines. Safety data sheets (SDS) for every product are kept in the binder at each line entrance and in the maintenance crib.

## 2. Chemical inventory

| Product | Use | Main hazard |
|---|---|---|
| Hydraulic oil ISO VG 46 | Press 4 hydraulic system | Slip hazard, skin irritation |
| Water-soluble cutting fluid | CNC machines | Skin irritation, mist |
| Solvent degreaser | Cleaning presses and fixtures | Flammable, eye and skin irritation |
| Lithium-complex grease NLGI 2 | Lubrication points | Skin irritation |

## 3. Storage

Keep solvent in approved flammable cabinets. Containers are labelled with the product name and hazard pictograms. Do not store more than 20 L of solvent at a line. Keep incompatible products in separate cabinets.

## 4. Spill response

1. Stop work and warn nearby people.
2. Eliminate ignition sources if the product is flammable.
3. For a small spill (less than 4 L) wear nitrile gloves and splash goggles, contain it with absorbent socks and cover it with absorbent pads.
4. Place used absorbent in the labelled waste container and wipe the floor until it is not slippery.
5. For a larger spill, or any spill that reaches a floor drain, evacuate the immediate area and call the plant emergency line on extension 2911.
6. Report the spill to the shift supervisor and EHS (extension 2305) before the end of the shift.

## 5. Eye and skin exposure

Flush eyes at the nearest eyewash station for at least 15 minutes. For skin contact, remove contaminated clothing and wash with soap and water. Call the first aid room on extension 2920 and bring the SDS for the product involved.
