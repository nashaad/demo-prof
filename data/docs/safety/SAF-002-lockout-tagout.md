# Control of Hazardous Energy (Lockout/Tagout)

Calder Ridge Manufacturing, Plant 1

## 1. Purpose and scope

This procedure sets out how energy sources are isolated before any employee enters the danger zone of a machine for servicing, cleaning, clearing a jam or adjustment. It applies to all machines on Lines 1, 2 and 3. Minor adjustments made during normal operation under approved safeguarding are covered by SAF-003.

## 2. Definitions

- **Authorized employee**: a person trained and signed off to apply lockout devices and tags. Each holds one personal lock and key.
- **Affected employee**: an operator who works near a machine that is being serviced.
- **Energy isolating device**: a disconnect, valve or blocking device that physically prevents the release of energy.
- **Zero energy state**: the condition in which all electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic, gravitational and stored energy has been isolated, released or restrained.

## 3. Energy sources by machine

| Machine | Sources | Isolating device |
|---|---|---|
| Press 4 (hydraulic, Line 3) | Electrical, hydraulic pump, accumulator, ram weight | Disconnect DS-4, accumulator dump valve, ram support blocks |
| Conveyor C2 (Line 3) | Electrical, pneumatic take-up cylinder | Disconnect DS-C2, air supply valve V-C2 |
| CNC 3 (Line 3) | Electrical, pneumatic, coolant pressure, pallet clamp hydraulics | Disconnect DS-CNC3, air drop valve, coolant pump breaker |

## 4. Applying lockout/tagout

1. Notify all affected employees that the machine will be shut down and isolated.
2. Shut the machine down using the normal stop control.
3. Operate each energy isolating device so that it is in the isolated position.
4. Apply a personal lockout device and a completed tag to each energy isolating device. The tag names the authorized employee, the date and the reason.
5. Release or restrain stored energy. Bleed the accumulator, vent trapped air, and lower the ram to its stop or install the ram support blocks.
6. Verify the zero energy state as described in section 5.

## 5. Verification of zero energy state

Before entering the danger zone, the authorized employee confirms that isolation is effective:

1. Clear all persons from the danger zone.
2. Attempt a normal start at the operator controls and confirm that nothing moves.
3. Check that pressure gauges read zero and that the ram, rollers or spindle are at rest.
4. Return all controls to the off position. The lockout stays on.

## 6. Removing lockout and restarting

1. Remove tools and replace all guards.
2. Confirm that all persons are clear and that affected employees are notified.
3. Each authorized employee removes their own lock and tag. A lock may not be removed by anyone else except under the supervisor removal procedure approved by EHS.
4. Re-energize and run a no-load test cycle before returning to production.

## 7. Group lockout and shift change

When several authorized employees work on one machine, each applies a personal lock to the group lockout box. Work that continues across a shift change requires the incoming authorized employee to apply their lock before the outgoing employee removes theirs, with a documented handover.
