# Dimensional Inspection Standard

Calder Ridge Manufacturing, Plant 1

## 1. Purpose and scope

This standard sets the dimensional requirements and inspection methods for machined and pressed parts produced on Lines 1, 2 and 3. It applies to first-article inspection, in-process checks and final inspection. Sampling frequencies are set in QC-004.

## 2. Inspection methods and equipment

| Feature | Method | Equipment |
|---|---|---|
| Outside diameter, length | Direct measurement | Digital caliper, 0.01 mm resolution |
| Bore diameter | Direct measurement | Three-point bore gauge, 0.001 mm resolution |
| Form and position | Coordinate measurement | CMM in the quality lab |

The gauge resolution must be at least one tenth of the feature tolerance. All gauges carry a calibration label that is in date (QC-003).

## 3. Part specification table

| Part number | Feature | Nominal | Tolerance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1124 | Bore diameter | 25.000 mm | +0.033 / -0.000 mm |
| 1138 | Outside diameter | 32.000 mm | +/- 0.050 mm |
| 1142 | Bore diameter | 25.000 mm | +0.021 / -0.000 mm |
| 1142 | Overall length | 80.00 mm | +/- 0.10 mm |
| 1147 | Bore diameter | 28.000 mm | +0.021 / -0.000 mm |
| 1204 | Flange thickness | 6.00 mm | +/- 0.05 mm |

For part number 1142 the bore diameter is 25.000 mm with a tolerance of +0.021 / -0.000 mm. An acceptable bore measures from 25.000 mm to 25.021 mm at 20 C.

## 4. First-article inspection

1. The operator machines the first part after setup or a tool change and delivers it to the quality lab.
2. The quality technician measures every feature in the table in section 3 and records the values against the part number.
3. Production starts only after the technician signs the first-article record.
4. Parts are measured at 20 C. Allow parts to stabilize in the lab for at least 30 minutes after machining.

## 5. Out-of-tolerance results

If a measurement falls outside the tolerance:

1. Stop production of that part number.
2. Quarantine the parts made since the last good measurement and follow QC-002.
3. Notify the quality technician on shift.
4. Do not adjust the tolerance, re-measure with a different gauge to find a passing value, or release parts without a disposition.

## 6. Measurement records

Record the part number, feature, measured value, gauge ID and inspector initials. Keep records for 7 years. Records for critical features carry the revision of this standard that applied on the measurement date.
