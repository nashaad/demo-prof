# CNC 3 Spindle and Workholding Maintenance

Calder Ridge Manufacturing, Plant 1

## 1. Scope

CNC 3 is the horizontal machining center on Line 3 that finishes the parts delivered by Conveyor C2. This manual covers the spindle, coolant system and pallet workholding. Lubrication intervals are in MNT-004. Inspection fixtures used by Quality are controlled by QC-003.

## 2. Spindle and coolant checks

| Check | Interval | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Spindle warm-up cycle | Every cold start | 10 minutes at 30 percent of maximum speed |
| Coolant concentration | Weekly | 8 to 10 percent by refractometer |
| Coolant filter differential pressure | Weekly | Replace the filter above 1.5 bar |
| Spindle taper cleaning | Daily | No chips or film on the taper |
| Tool changer alignment check | Every 6 months | Maintenance technician sign-off |

## 3. Pallet fixtures and clamp inspection

Each pallet fixture uses FC-210 fixture clamps. Inspect all clamps at every fixture change and at least once a month.

1. Look for cracked clamp bodies, stretched bolts and damaged threads.
2. Replace any clamp bolt that has been tightened more than 25 times.
3. Check that clamp faces are flat to within 0.05 mm.
4. Remove burrs and chips from the pallet locating bores before mounting a fixture.

## 4. Fixture clamp torque

Fixture clamp FC-210 uses M10 class 10.9 clamp bolts. Tighten in two passes, in a star pattern, using a calibrated torque wrench:

1. First pass: 25 Nm on every bolt.
2. Final pass: 45 Nm on every bolt.

Mark each bolt head with a torque stripe after the final pass. A broken stripe means the bolt has moved and must be retorqued. Do not use an impact driver on FC-210 clamp bolts.

## 5. Records

Record the fixture number, the clamp serial numbers and the final torque value on the fixture change sheet. Torque wrenches used for this task are calibrated under QC-003.
