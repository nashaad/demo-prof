# Lubrication Schedule for Line 3 Equipment

Calder Ridge Manufacturing, Plant 1

## 1. Scope

This schedule lists the lubrication points, lubricants and intervals for the equipment on Line 3: Press 4, Conveyor C2 and CNC 3. Intervals are in operating hours, read from each machine's hour meter, unless a calendar interval is given. This revision replaces rev A.

## 2. Approved lubricants

| Code | Lubricant | Used on |
|---|---|---|
| G-2 | Lithium-complex grease, NLGI 2 | Press 4 gibs and bearings, Conveyor C2 bearings |
| H-46 | Hydraulic oil, ISO VG 46 | Press 4 hydraulic system |
| GO-220 | Synthetic gear oil, ISO VG 220 | Conveyor C2 gearbox |
| W-68 | Way lube, ISO VG 68 | CNC 3 slideways |

Do not mix lubricants. Clean the grease nipple before each application.

## 3. Press 4 schedule

| Point | Lubricant | Interval |
|---|---|---|
| Ram guide gibs (8 points) | G-2 | Every 24 operating hours |
| Crown main bearings (2 points) | G-2 | Every 250 operating hours |
| Hydraulic oil level | H-46 | Daily check, top up as needed |

Apply 3 pumps of grease per gib point with the hand gun. Wipe off the excess.

## 4. Conveyor C2 schedule

| Point | Lubricant | Interval |
|---|---|---|
| Head pulley bearings (2 points) | G-2 | Every 160 operating hours |
| Tail pulley bearings (2 points) | G-2 | Every 160 operating hours |
| Gearbox oil level check | GO-220 | Monthly |
| Gearbox oil change | GO-220 | Every 4,000 operating hours |

## 5. CNC 3 schedule

| Point | Lubricant | Interval |
|---|---|---|
| Slideway lubricator reservoir | W-68 | Weekly check, refill below the minimum mark |
| Ballscrews (X, Y, Z) | Grease per the machine plate | Every 6 months |
| Pallet changer rails | W-68 | Monthly |

## 6. Records

Technicians initial the lubrication route card at each point. The planner reviews missed points at the weekly maintenance meeting. Lockout/tagout under SAF-002 applies before lubricating any point inside a guard.

## 7. Changes from rev A

The Press 4 ram guide gib interval changed from every 40 operating hours to every 24 operating hours, following gib wear found at the last two preventive services. No other intervals changed.
