# Press 4 Hydraulic Press Preventive Maintenance

Calder Ridge Manufacturing, Plant 1

## 1. Scope

This manual covers preventive maintenance of Press 4, the 400-ton hydraulic press on Line 3. Lubrication points and greasing intervals are listed separately in MNT-004. Safety requirements for guarding and lockout apply to every task in this manual (SAF-002, SAF-003).

## 2. Service intervals

| Task | Interval | Performed by |
|---|---|---|
| Operator walk-around and leak check | Every shift | Operator |
| Hydraulic oil level and temperature check | Daily | Operator |
| Hydraulic oil sample for analysis | Every 250 operating hours | Maintenance |
| Preventive service (section 3) | Every 500 operating hours or 90 days, whichever comes first | Maintenance |
| Accumulator precharge check | Every 6 months | Maintenance |
| Hydraulic oil change | Every 12 months | Maintenance |

The operating hour count is read from the hour meter on the Press 4 control panel. When the 90 day limit is reached first, the preventive service is still performed in full.

## 3. Preventive service tasks

Apply lockout/tagout under SAF-002 and verify the zero energy state before starting.

1. Inspect all hoses, fittings and the ram seal area for weeping or wear. Replace any hose with surface cracking.
2. Replace the return line filter element (10 micron) and the pressure filter element.
3. Check the hydraulic oil level in the reservoir. Top up with ISO VG 46 hydraulic oil only. Reservoir capacity is 400 L.
4. Check the pump drive coupling for wear and alignment.
5. Check the ram parallelism to the bolster. The maximum deviation is 0.10 mm across the bolster.
6. Inspect the light curtain mounting and the guard windows. Report any damage under SAF-003 section 4.
7. Run a no-load cycle after reassembly and check for leaks.

## 4. Operating specifications

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Rated force | 400 tons |
| System pressure | 280 bar |
| Accumulator nitrogen precharge | 100 bar at 20 C |
| Oil temperature, normal range | 35 to 55 C |
| Oil temperature, shutdown limit | 65 C |

If the oil temperature reaches the shutdown limit, stop the press at the end of the cycle and call maintenance on extension 2450.

## 5. Records

Record each service in the maintenance system with the hour meter reading, the filter part numbers installed and the oil sample reference. The planner schedules the next preventive service from the hour meter reading or the 90 day date, whichever is earlier.
