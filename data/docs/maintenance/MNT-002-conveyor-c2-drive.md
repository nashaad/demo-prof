# Conveyor C2 Drive Assembly Maintenance

Calder Ridge Manufacturing, Plant 1

## 1. Scope

Conveyor C2 carries parts from Press 4 to CNC 3 on Line 3. This manual covers the drive assembly: the 2.2 kW gearmotor, the flexible jaw coupling, the gearbox and the head pulley. Lubrication intervals are in MNT-004.

## 2. Drive assembly components

The gearmotor is mounted on a common baseplate with the gearbox. A flexible jaw coupling with a polyurethane spider connects the gearmotor shaft to the gearbox input shaft. The gearbox output drives the head pulley through a keyed shaft. Disconnect DS-C2 isolates the electrical supply and valve V-C2 isolates the air supply to the take-up cylinder.

## 3. Torque specifications

Use a calibrated torque wrench. Tighten bolts in a star pattern where there are more than two.

| Fastener | Size and class | Torque |
|---|---|---|
| Drive coupling bolts | M12, class 8.8 | 80 Nm (59 ft-lb) |
| Gearbox mounting bolts | M16, class 8.8 | 120 Nm (89 ft-lb) |
| Gearmotor mounting bolts | M10, class 8.8 | 35 Nm (26 ft-lb) |
| Take-up screw locknuts | M8, class 8.8 | 22 Nm (16 ft-lb) |
| Head pulley bearing housing bolts | M12, class 8.8 | 75 Nm (55 ft-lb) |

Retorque the drive coupling bolts after the first 8 hours of running following any coupling replacement.

## 4. Coupling inspection

Inspect the coupling every 3 months, or whenever the conveyor makes a knocking noise.

1. Apply lockout/tagout under SAF-002 and verify the zero energy state.
2. Remove the coupling guard.
3. Check the spider for cracking, hardening or loss of material. Replace the spider if the jaws can be rotated by hand against each other by more than 1.5 mm.
4. Check alignment with a dial indicator. Maximum parallel misalignment is 0.15 mm and maximum angular misalignment is 0.5 degrees.
5. Check the drive coupling bolts for the torque in section 3.
6. Refit the coupling guard before removing the lockout.

## 5. Belt tracking and tension

Belt tension is set with the take-up screws so that the belt sags 10 to 15 mm at mid-span under a 5 kg load. Adjust the take-up screws evenly on both sides, then tighten the locknuts to the value in section 3. Run the belt empty for 10 minutes and confirm that it tracks within 5 mm of the centre line of the pulley.

## 6. Abnormal conditions

| Symptom | Likely cause | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Knocking at start-up | Worn coupling spider | Replace spider, check alignment |
| Gearbox hot to touch (above 80 C) | Low oil or overload | Stop, check oil level, call maintenance |
| Belt drifts to one side | Uneven take-up | Re-adjust take-up screws |
